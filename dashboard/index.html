    Risk & opportunity the three that matter most this period

    ⚠ Milk pricing moved fast

    Up 46.1% in 12 weeks — the sharpest price move in the data. Grocery demand overall is only mildly price-sensitive, so this is worth confirming isn't a pricing error before it's treated as strategy.

    ⚠ Kiosk & Gas is promo-dependent

    94.6% of its sales happen at a discount — the highest of any department. Worth a manual margin check before scaling promotions further here.

    ↗ Beef price cut looks like a giveaway

    Beef fell 16.3% in 12 weeks, but demand here barely responds to price — a 1% price cut buys roughly half a percent more volume. More likely eroding margin than winning share.
